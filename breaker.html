Privacy Policy: Breaker

Package ID: com.shahforge.breaker · Effective Date: 26 November 2026
v1.0 · Offline & Ad-Free

1. Overview

Breaker is developed and published by ShahForge. Version 1.0 of Breaker is a self-contained, offline, ad-free game that does not collect personal information.

2. Information We Collect

Version 1.0 of Breaker collects no personal data, usage analytics, crash telemetry, precise or coarse location data, or advertising identifiers.

  • No user account or sign-in is required.
  • No data is transmitted to ShahForge servers or third-party trackers.

3. Local Device Storage

Your campaign progress, S-Coins balance, tool upgrade levels, achievements, and audio/haptic settings are stored strictly locally on your device. You can erase all local progress at any time through Settings → Reset Campaign Progress inside the app, or by uninstalling the game.

4. Advertising & Future Updates (v2.0+)

Version 1.0 contains no advertisements and bundles no advertising SDKs. If a future version (such as Version 2.0) introduces optional advertising services like Google AdMob, those third-party SDKs may process device identifiers (such as Google Advertising ID) and diagnostic signals to deliver and measure ads. We will update this Privacy Policy and the app store Data Safety disclosures before any version containing ads is published.

5. Children's Privacy

Breaker Version 1.0 does not collect, store, or transmit personal data from children or any other users.

6. Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, please reach out to ShahForge via the developer contact email shown on the Breaker store listing or via GitHub (shahforge1).
